RosterView — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

The short version. Your roster is stored on your phone. Your airline password is never seen by RosterView. Nothing is shared with other pilots unless you switch sharing on yourself — and it is off by default.

Your airline login

RosterView opens your airline's own website inside the app so you can sign in there directly. Your airline username, password and any image or code verification are handled entirely by that website. RosterView does not read, record, store or transmit them, and they are never sent to our servers.

Your roster

When you import, your roster is read from the page and stored on your device. A copy of the original roster page is also saved on your device, under "Original", so you can check the app's times against the source. Both stay on the phone and are removed when you delete the app.

Sharing with other pilots (optional)

Sharing is off unless you create an account and choose to turn it on. Your visibility setting controls everything:

  • Private (default) — your roster is not uploaded at all. It stays on your phone. If you were previously sharing and switch to Private, the copy on our server is deleted.
  • Favourites — your roster is stored on our server and can only be read by pilots whose request you have accepted.
  • Open — your roster can be read by any signed-in RosterView pilot.

These rules are enforced by the database itself, not merely by the app.

Your account

If you create an account we store your email address, an encrypted password, and the profile you enter (name, staff number, rank, and a photo if you add one). Authentication and storage are provided by Supabase. Your email is never shown to other pilots.

Other people's names

Airline rosters list the crew you are flying with. Those names are stored on your device as part of your roster, and are included if you choose to share your roster. Please treat colleagues' details with the same care you would the printed roster.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party trackers, and no selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No location collection.
  • No access to your contacts, photos or calendar (a photo is read only if you pick one).

Deleting your data

  • Account and shared rosters: in the app, tap your photo → Profile & settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, any rosters you shared and your connections with other pilots.
  • Everything on the phone: delete the app.

Children

RosterView is intended for professional airline crew and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
